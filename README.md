SleepSync: Personal Sleep Intelligence

SleepSync is a web application that helps you track your sleep, understand your sleep habits, and build a healthier bedtime routine. It runs entirely in the browser, with no installation, no account and no server.

Live demo: https://shah-hussainn.github.io/SleepSync /

Features
Dashboard: shows a recommended bedtime and wake-up time, your sleep score, sleep debt, average sleep over the last 7 days, the number of nights logged, and a list of recent nights.
Log Sleep: record the date, bedtime, wake-up time, a 1–5 star sleep quality rating and optional notes. The sleep duration is calculated automatically as you type.
Insights: personalized feedback based on your logs, including average sleep, average quality, and your personal sleep need.
Alarms: set a wake-up alarm and a bedtime reminder, with a test notification button.
Settings: enter your age, chronotype, usual wake time and target sleep hours so the recommendations fit you. You can also clear all saved data.
Saved data: your logs and settings stay in the browser (localStorage) and are still there after you reload the page.

How to Run

Option 1: Online Open the live demo link above.

Option 2: On your computer

Download or clone this repository.
Open index.html in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).

An internet connection is only needed for the fonts. If you are offline, the app still works with default fonts.

How to Use
Open Log Sleep and enter last night's bedtime, wake time and quality rating, then click Save Sleep Log.
Repeat for a few nights. Insights improve as you log more nights.
Check the Dashboard for your score, sleep debt and recommended schedule.
Open Settings to enter your age and target sleep hours for better recommendations.
In Alarms, turn on the alarm or bedtime reminder and allow notifications when your browser asks.
Notes
All data stays on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.
Data is stored per browser, so logs entered in one browser or device will not appear in another.
Alarms and reminders work while the page is open in the browser.
